英：Among the 47 Builders, the top three problems were keeping progress steady, getting results to make a difference, and a few people holding everything up. “Not finding partners”, the one most people would think of first, came only fourth.

**[ch3-1-03]**
中：這部分題目只由做過專案的 47 人回答。問卷列出 13 種常見困難請填答者評分，分析時看兩個指標：評 3 分以上（3、4、5 分合計）代表問題真的卡住進度；評 4 分以上（4、5 分合計）代表嚴重到讓人想退出。
英：Only the 47 Builders answered these questions. The survey listed 13 common difficulties and asked them to rate each one. We use two measures: a rating of 3 or higher (3, 4 and 5 combined) means the problem really held back progress; 4 or higher (4 and 5 combined) means it was bad enough to make them want to quit.

**[ch3-1-04]**
中：**困擾程度前三名是專案難有穩定進度、成果沒有著力點、少數人硬撐；一般最容易想到的「找不到願意一起做的夥伴」排在第四。**這三件事可能相互影響：節奏斷掉讓成果遲遲出不來，成果出不來就更難證明專案值得投入，願意接手的人隨之變少，最後重擔又回到少數人身上。
英：**The top three problems were keeping progress steady, getting results to make a difference, and a few people holding everything up. “Can’t find partners who want to work on it together”, the one most people would think of first, came fourth.** These three may feed into each other: when momentum breaks, results are slow to appear; without results, it is harder to show the project is worth the effort; fewer people step in, and the load falls back on the few.

**[ch3-2-01]**
中：3.2 決定權越大的人，越容易累垮
英：3.2 The more say people have, the more likely they are to burn out

**[ch3-2-02]**（重點卡）
中：在做過專案的填答者中有一項傾向：決定權越大，感到累垮的比例越高。自己起頭發起專案的 10 人中，有 6 人（60%）表示核心成員累垮明顯卡住進度。
英：Among the Builders there is a pattern: the more say someone has in a project, the more likely they are to feel burned out. Of the 10 who started their own project, 6 (60%) said core-member burnout clearly held back progress.

**[ch3-2-03]**（表格）
中：表格標題：你在專案裡的位置與決定權大小
英：Table: Your position in the project and how much say you have

| Your position in the project | Say in direction | Core members burning out | Results struggle to make a difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Joined someone else’s project; the founder sets the direction | Least | 12% | 12% |
| Define the problem and design the solution with others | Shared | 33% | 44% |
| Started it myself: spotted the problem, found people, got it going | Most | 60% | 50% |

**[ch3-2-04]**
中：從這次填答者的傾向來看，專案裡參與越深、決定權越大的成員，有更高比例面臨核心成員累垮（很可能就是自己），也更擔心專案成果難以發揮影響力。
英：Among these respondents, those more deeply involved and with more say were more likely to face core-member burnout (quite likely their own), and more worried that the results would not make a difference.

**[ch3-3-01]**
中：3.3 站在決策位置的人，看不見新手的困境
英：3.3 Decision-makers don’t see what newcomers struggle with

**[ch3-3-02]**（重點卡）
中：規劃「如何讓新手參與」時，光問發起專案、有決定權的人可能問不出答案：因為那通常不是他們自己遇到的困難。
英：When planning how to bring newcomers in, asking only founders and decision-makers may not get you the answer: it is usually not a problem they face themselves.

**[ch3-3-03]**
中：反過來看「新手或不會寫程式的人，不知道能幫什麼」這題，比較有感的反而落在參與較淺的一端：主動通報 6 人中有 2 人（33%）、接收資訊 5 人中有 1 人（20%）、加入別人專案 8 人中有 1 人（12%）、共創解法組 0%（0/18）、發起專案 10 人中有 1 人（10%）。
英：Now look at the item “Newcomers or people who can’t code don’t know how they can help”. The people who feel it most are at the shallow end: 2 of 6 (33%) in the Report group, 1 of 5 (20%) in the Follow group, 1 of 8 (12%) who joined someone else’s project, 0% (0/18) in the Co-design group, and 1 of 10 (10%) who started a project.

**[ch3-3-04]**
中：參與程度較淺的人，有較高比例擔心新手不寫程式能幫上什麼忙；隨著在專案裡的決定權提高，這層擔憂比例大致呈現較低傾向。**因此若要規劃「怎麼讓新手參與」，光問發起專案、對專案有決定權的人可能問不出答案，因為那不是他們自己會遇到的困難。**
英：People less involved were more likely to worry about how newcomers who can’t code could help. As people’s say in a project grows, this worry broadly tends to be lower. **So if you want to plan how to bring newcomers in, asking only founders and decision-makers may not get you the answer, because it is not a problem they face themselves.**

**[ch3-4-01]**
中：3.4 面對困難時，實際採取了什麼行動？
英：3.4 What did people actually do about these difficulties?

**[ch3-4-02]**（重點卡）
中：最常見的兩種應對方向完全相反：一種是向外求助，另一種是放慢步調、降低期待。在回答的 28 人中，有 11 人選擇主動降低期待，或沒有特別採取行動。
英：The two most common responses pointed in opposite directions: reaching out for help, or slowing down and lowering expectations. Of the 28 who answered, 11 chose to lower their expectations or took no particular action.

**[ch3-4-03]**（表格，內容由程式產生）

**[ch3-4-04]**
中：問卷另外以選填題詢問「面對這些困難，你有採取什麼行動去解決嗎」，有 28 人填寫。
英：The survey also asked an optional question, “Did you take any action to deal with these difficulties?” 28 people answered.

**[ch3-4-05]**
中：最常見的兩種應對方向完全相反：一種是向外求助（6 人），另一種是放慢步調、降低期待（5 人），後者多偏向順其自然、不強求進度。加上未特別行動或只描述困難的 6 人，28 位填答者中有 11 人屬於主動降低期待或未特別處理。
英：The two most common responses pointed in opposite directions: reaching out for help (6 people), or slowing down and lowering expectations (5 people). The second group mostly let things take their course rather than push for progress. Adding the 6 who took no particular action or only described the difficulty, 11 of the 28 either chose to lower their expectations or did nothing in particular.
