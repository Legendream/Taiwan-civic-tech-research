## Your next step

Most of these pages are in Mandarin.

### 🌱 Still looking for a way in
- [Browse projects on Civic Tech Taiwan](https://civictech.tw/): start with an issue you care about
- [Check upcoming hackathons](https://g0v.hackmd.io/@jothon/event_promote_rundown): ask people in person and join in on the day
- [Join g0v's Slack](https://g0v.hackmd.io/@daisuke/ryjkbFyuS): find the channels where people are actively working

- [Pitch at a hackathon to find teammates](https://g0v.hackmd.io/@jothon/event_promote_rundown): three minutes on stage to say who you need
- [Use this survey's raw data in your proposal](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Z7am3yalgPbQLureQS0tWTofSKrLJRAI4473pFLErag/edit?gid=780169479#gid=780169479): open data, CC BY 4.0

### 💡 Looking to collaborate
- [See what help projects are asking for](https://civictech.tw/#wish-pool): each project idea lists the skills it needs
- [Meet teams at a hackathon](https://g0v.hackmd.io/@jothon/event_promote_rundown): introducing experts, connecting a team with government contacts or offering a meeting space all help
