## 四、參與深度五級（問卷選項）⏳

問卷選項是一整句描述，圖表用短名稱。依「對專案方向的決定權」由小到大：

| 中文短名 | 英文短名 | 問卷選項原意（英譯） |
|---|---|---|
| 接收資訊 | Follow | I care about some issues and read related information or results, but rarely do hands-on work myself |
| 主動通報 | Report | I provide information when a project asks for it, e.g. reporting road conditions or uploading pollution data |
| 協力參與 | Join | I join projects others started and help get things done; I rarely take part in deciding the project's direction |
| 共創解法 | Co-design | I work with others to define the problem clearly and design how to solve it; my actions shape the project's direction |
| 發起專案 | Start | I am often the one who gets things started: I spot the problem, find partners and launch the project |
| 以上都不太像我 | None of these fits me | 完整選項：以上都不太像我，我以別的形式接觸公民科技專案（None of these fits me; I engage with civic tech projects in another way） |
