## 英文正文

# Who Builds Civic Tech in Taiwan?

**What 127 people around g0v told us about getting in, getting stuck, and staying**

**Picture a g0v hackathon.** About 100 people from different cities, backgrounds and ages fill a room. Anyone can take three minutes to pitch an idea, then find people to build it with. g0v holds one every two months.

g0v is a grassroots civic tech community founded in Taiwan. In 2026, g0v Jothon, the team that organizes these hackathons, ran a survey to learn who takes part and what gets in their way. This page sums up what 127 people told us.

## The short version

At every hackathon, many projects are looking for partners. But for people who have actually worked on a project, finding partners is not the hardest part. The hardest part is keeping progress steady, making the results count, and core members burning out.

## Three groups, three stages

We sorted the 127 people by how close they are to civic tech:

| Group | People | Where they are |
|---|---|---|
| Heard of it | 26 | Have heard of or seen civic tech, but not taken part |
| Been around | 54 | Know the community, but have not worked on a project |
| Built something | 47 | Have worked on at least one civic tech project |

Questions about real project experience, such as what gets stuck and what keeps people going, were answered only by the 47 who built something.

## 四原則怎麼用在這兩節

- **精簡**：沒有放數字百分比；三群只寫人數；「為什麼要做調查」壓成半句
- **具體化**：用黑客松現場開場（官網的真實描述，不是虛構情境）
- **分類**：三群人用表格對比，各取一個好記的名字
- **由簡入繁**：現場畫面 → g0v 是什麼 → 一句話結論 → 三群人；細節留給後面各節
